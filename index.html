From money deployed to value today

Deployed since inception is every dirham the fund has put into deals since May-25. When a deal repays, that money is lent again, so this total is larger than the members' capital.

At cost is what is still out in deals: deployed less capital returned (and anything written off).

Value now is at cost, except gold and silver, which are valued at the market price on the reporting date. Land is held at cost until it is sold or earns income. Finance profit counts only when received.
